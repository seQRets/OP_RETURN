Does the builder actually work?

Opening this page runs the real builder, invisibly, and puts it through every check below. Typing messages, pasting things into the wrong box, going over the size limit. Green means it did the right thing just now, on your computer, in your own browser.

You do not have to understand every line. If the bar below is green, the tool behaved. If it is ever red, something is broken and the builder should not be trusted until it is fixed.
